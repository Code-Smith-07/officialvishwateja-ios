Privacy Policy

P2P Radio · Last updated: 7 November 2026 · Developer: Vishwateja S B (Official Vishwateja)

1. The short version

P2P Radio turns your iPhone, iPad or Android phone into a push-to-talk radio. Your voice goes directly from one of your devices to the other, encrypted. We do not run a server, and we do not collect, receive or store any of your data.

  • Voice travels device to device, encrypted, and is never recorded or saved
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking
  • The microphone is on only while you hold the talk button, or while you turn on Lock

2. What we collect

Nothing. Nothing from P2P Radio is ever sent to us. What the app itself keeps, all of it on your device:

  • Your radio identity: a random key pair made on your device. Its public half becomes your radio ID, which other radios use to recognise yours
  • Your paired radios: the name, radio ID and public key of each radio you paired or joined a room with, so they can reconnect by themselves
  • Your room code and radio name, if you set them
  • Your settings, such as haptics
  • A short connection log on your device for troubleshooting. It holds connection states and counts only: never audio, never addresses, never keys

3. What is stored and where

  • Your radio identity, paired radios and room code are kept in the secure storage of your device (the iOS Keychain, or the Android Keystore)
  • Forget removes a radio. Change code makes a new room code
  • Deleting the app removes its data. On iPhone and iPad, iOS can keep Keychain items after an app is deleted, so a reinstalled app may still recognise your paired radios
  • We keep no copy of any of this, because we never receive it

4. Network access

P2P Radio connects your devices to each other. It never contacts a server of ours. Connections it makes:

  • Voice and talk signals go directly between your devices, encrypted (WebRTC with DTLS-SRTP). The other device learns your network address, as any direct connection needs
  • On the same Wi-Fi, the app announces your radio on the local network (Bonjour) with your radio name, radio ID, local network address and whether you have a room open. Other devices on that Wi-Fi can see your radio name. Joining a room needs its code, and paired radios prove who they are with their keys before they connect
  • Across the internet, to find a path between two networks the app asks public STUN servers run by Google and Cloudflare for your public network address. Like any internet request, those servers see your IP address
  • Automatic invite (optional): if you link two radios over the internet with the automatic invite instead of scanning codes both ways, the setup messages go through ntfy.sh, a public relay. They are encrypted on your device, so ntfy.sh cannot read them; it sees your IP address and a random channel name, and keeps the messages for a short time under its own policy. Manual pairing by QR code uses no relay
  • No relay server carries your voice, and nothing is uploaded for storage

Google, Cloudflare and ntfy.sh have their own privacy policies.

5. Permissions we request

  • Microphone: to send your voice, only while you hold the talk button or have Lock on. The app shows that you are on air, and Lock turns itself off after 10 minutes
  • Camera: only to scan pairing and room QR codes. Images are not saved
  • Local network: to find and connect to your radios on the same Wi-Fi
  • Background audio: so a linked radio keeps working with the screen off. Disconnect stops it
  • Android only: notifications (to show that the radio is linked) and Bluetooth (to play through your headset)

We do not ask for your contacts, location, photos or files.

6. What we do not do

  • We never record or store your voice
  • We do not sell, share or rent any data, because we have none
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind
  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data. The WebRTC audio library is open source and runs on your device

7. Children

P2P Radio is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app only links radios that someone paired or let into a room with its code; it has no public chat and no way to find strangers.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page and the "Last updated" date changes. If a change is important, it is also mentioned in the update notes.
