Privacy Policy

Daily Diary Notes · Last updated: August 3, 2026 · Developer: Vishwateja S B (Official Vishwateja)

1. The short version

Daily Diary Notes collects nothing. It has no account, no sign-in, no server and no analytics. It makes no network requests of any kind — not on first launch, not ever.

Your entries, photographs, moods and settings are files on your own device. Nobody else can read them, including the developer.

2. What we collect

Nothing. There is no personal information, no analytics, no usage data and no identifiers. There are no accounts, no sign-ups and no third-party SDKs of any kind.

3. What is stored, and where

Everything the app keeps, it keeps on your device:

  • Your journals and entries, as files in the app's private storage.
  • Photographs you attach to an entry, and any background image you choose for the home screen, copied into that same private storage.
  • Preferences such as your reading size, reminder time and chosen binding, in the app's own settings.

All of it is removed when you delete the app.

4. Permissions we request

  • Photographs — asked for only when you attach a picture to an entry or choose your own home screen background. The app reads the picture you pick and nothing else.
  • Face ID or Touch ID — asked for only if you turn on the lock in Settings. Authentication is handled entirely by iOS; the app is told yes or no and never sees your biometric data.
  • Notifications — asked for only if you turn on the daily reminder. The reminder is scheduled on your device and its text never leaves it.
  • Microphone — the current version includes a usage description for a planned voice-note feature, but it does not request microphone access, record audio or store audio.

5. Backups

If you have iCloud Backup or encrypted device backups turned on in iOS, your diary is included in those backups, the same as any other app's data. That is between you and Apple; the app neither requests it nor has access to it.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store or transmit your personal data.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • We do not show advertising in the app.
  • We do not read, sync or back up your entries to any server — we operate none.
  • We do not include a single third-party SDK.

7. Children

The app is rated 4+ and is safe for any age. Because it collects nothing from anybody, it collects nothing from children either.

8. Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new "Last updated" date. Since there is no account, there is no way to notify you directly — so any change that actually affects you will be described in the app's release notes as well.

9. Who publishes this app

Daily Diary Notes is published by Official Vishwateja, an independent developer — Vishwateja S B. There is no company network, no data team and no third party involved: the app is written, signed and submitted by one person.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy, or about anything in this policy, go to officialvishwateja@proton.me. The same address handles support — it is read by the developer rather than a helpdesk, and normally answered within two business days.

You can also visit the support page.

Bottom line: Your diary is a file on your phone and nowhere else. No accounts, no analytics, no cloud — there is nothing for us to see.
