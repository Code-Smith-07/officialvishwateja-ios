Privacy Policy

Notes X · Last updated: 20 November 2026 · Developer: Vishwateja S B (Official Vishwateja)

1. The short version

Notes X is a handwriting notebook for iPhone and iPad: paged notebooks, an endless canvas, PDF markup, audio linked to your ink, flashcards and 3D drawing. We do not collect, receive or store any of your data. Your notebooks live on your device, and in your own iCloud only if you turn on iCloud Sync.

  • Your notes, drawings, recordings and photos stay on your device or in your own iCloud
  • Handwriting search and the Study Helper run on your device. Nothing is uploaded
  • No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs

2. What we collect

Nothing. Nothing from Notes X is ever sent to us. What the app itself keeps, on your device:

  • Your notebooks: ink, pages, covers, stickers, tape, photos and PDFs you add, flashcards, pins and folders
  • Audio recordings, only the ones you make with Record, saved inside the notebook they belong to
  • A search index of the words read from your handwriting, so search is instant
  • Older versions of each notebook (the newest 20) and the backups you or the weekly automatic backup make
  • Your settings, today's focus timer count and your chosen language

3. What is stored and where

  • Notebooks are kept in the app's own storage on your device. Backups are saved in the Files app, Notes X, Backups, where you can see and delete them
  • If you turn on iCloud Sync, your notebooks are stored in your own iCloud Drive and synced by Apple between your devices. We cannot see them
  • Deleted notebooks stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days, then they are removed. Deleting the app removes everything stored on that device

We keep no copy of any of this, because we never receive it.

4. Network access

  • Notes X has no servers and never contacts any server of ours
  • iCloud Sync (optional): when you turn it on, Apple's iCloud moves your notebooks between your own devices
  • Purchases: Notes X Pro Yearly and Lifetime are bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details or your Apple Account
  • Apple Intelligence (Study Helper): summaries and quiz cards are made by Apple's on-device model. Your text is not sent anywhere
  • Links to this page and the support page open in your browser

5. Permissions we request

  • Microphone: only when you tap Record, so audio can play back with your ink. You can turn it off in Settings at any time
  • Camera: only in AR mode, so you can draw in the air and on surfaces around you. Nothing is recorded or saved
  • Add to Photos: only when you choose Save in the share sheet for an Ink Replay video or an exported page
  • Notifications: only for the Focus Timer, to tell you a session or break has ended
  • Adding a photo uses Apple's photo picker, so Notes X sees only the photos you pick and never your whole library

We do not ask for contacts, location, health data or tracking.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, share or rent any data, because we have none
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind
  • No accounts or sign-in
  • Your notes are never uploaded and never used to train AI models
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data

7. Children

Notes X is made for students and anyone who writes, and it is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Since nothing is collected, there is nothing to share.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page and the "Last updated" date changes. If a change is important, it is also mentioned in the update notes.

9. Who publishes this app

Notes X is published by Official Vishwateja, an independent developer: one person, Vishwateja S B. There is no company data team and there are no third parties involved.
