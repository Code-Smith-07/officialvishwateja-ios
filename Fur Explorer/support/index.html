Getting Started

  • Install: open Fur Explorer. It needs macOS 13 or newer on a Mac with Apple silicon
  • Add your files: click Add Folder or Drive and choose your home folder, a folder such as Pictures, or an external drive. Fur Explorer indexes it and remembers it
  • Browse: pick a category on the shelf at the top (Photos, Videos, Audio, Documents and more), or search by name
  • Preview: double-click or press Space to preview photos, videos (including MKV), music, PDFs and Office documents without opening another app

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Fur Explorer move or copy my files?

No. Your files stay where they are. They change only when you ask, for example to rename, move, copy or move a file to the Trash. Renames and moves on the same drive can be undone with Command-Z or from File Activity.

Why don't I see all my files?

Fur Explorer shows the locations you added. Very small photos, videos and music are filtered out by default so the library is not full of icons and clips; change this in Settings, Indexing. System folders, hidden folders and app packages are skipped.

A video plays without sound

Most video and sound formats play inside the app, including DTS and MP3 sound in MKV files. A few sound formats cannot be played by macOS; the preview says so, and Open in default app plays the file in another player.

How do Hidden Items work?

Right-click a file or folder and choose Hide from Fur Explorer. It leaves the library and appears only in Hidden Items (View, Hidden Items, or Shift-Command-H), which opens with Touch ID. On a Mac without Touch ID it opens with your Mac login password. Unhide puts it back.

How do I find lyrics or subtitles?

Open a song or video and press Search under Lyrics or Subtitles. Lyrics come from LRCLIB. Subtitles come from OpenSubtitles and need your own OpenSubtitles API key. Nothing is sent until you press Search.

How do I remove a location or start over?

Open Indexed Locations and choose Exclude on a location to remove it and its catalogue. Your files are not touched. Deleting the app and its folder removes everything.
