Privacy Policy

Fur Explorer · Last updated: 24 October 2026 · Developer: Vishwateja S B (Official Vishwateja)

1. The short version

Fur Explorer is a Mac app that organises the photos, videos, music and documents already on your Mac and drives, and lets you browse, search and preview them. We do not collect, receive or store any of your data. Its file catalogue is built and kept on your Mac.

  • Your files stay where they are. Fur Explorer reads them on your Mac and never uploads them
  • No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs
  • The only internet use is the optional lyrics and subtitle search, and only after you press Search

2. What we collect

Nothing. Nothing from Fur Explorer is ever sent to us. What the app itself keeps, all of it on your Mac:

  • A catalogue of your files: names, folders, sizes, dates and types of the files in the locations you index, so browsing and search are instant
  • Thumbnails of photos, videos, music artwork and documents, so they show quickly
  • Folder access: macOS bookmarks for the folders and drives you add, so you do not have to choose them again
  • Your pins, recent files, file activity and hidden-item list
  • Your settings

3. What is stored and where

  • The catalogue, thumbnails and settings are kept in Fur Explorer's own folder in your Mac account (inside the app's sandbox container for the Mac App Store version)
  • Removing a location in Indexed Locations deletes its catalogue records and thumbnails. Deleting the app and its folder removes everything
  • Your original files are never copied into the app. They change only when you ask, for example to rename, move, copy or move a file to the Trash

We keep no copy of any of this, because we never receive it.

4. Network access

  • Lyrics search (optional): when you press Search on a song, the title and artist text you see goes to LRCLIB (lrclib.net) to find its lyrics
  • Subtitle search (optional): when you press Search on a video, the title text and language go to OpenSubtitles (opensubtitles.com), using the OpenSubtitles API key you enter. Downloading a subtitle may count against your OpenSubtitles account. Your key and login are used for that request only and are not saved
  • Your audio and video files themselves are never uploaded. Nothing else connects to the internet, and the app never contacts any server of ours
  • Those two services have their own privacy policies and see the usual connection details of a request, such as your IP address

5. Permissions we request

  • Folders and drives you choose: the Mac App Store version reads only the locations you add with Add Folder or Drive, as macOS requires
  • Full Disk Access (direct download version only): turned on by you in System Settings, so it can organise your whole Mac and connected drives. You can turn it off at any time
  • Touch ID: to open Hidden Items. macOS checks your fingerprint and only tells the app yes or no; your fingerprint never reaches the app. A Mac without Touch ID uses your Mac login password, also checked by macOS
  • Outgoing network connections: only for the optional lyrics and subtitle search above

We do not ask for the camera, microphone, contacts, location, screen recording or accessibility.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, share or rent any data, because we have none
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind
  • No accounts or sign-in
  • Your files are never uploaded and never used to train AI models
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data

7. Children

Fur Explorer is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Since nothing is collected, there is nothing to share.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page and the "Last updated" date changes. If a change is important, it is also mentioned in the update notes.

9. Who publishes this app

Fur Explorer is published by Official Vishwateja, an independent developer: one person, Vishwateja S B. There is no company data team and there are no third parties involved.
