Getting Started

  • Start a notebook: tap Start a Notebook and choose Pages, Canvas (endless) or 3D Space, then a cover and paper. You can also import a PDF to write on
  • Write: your pens are Apple's own pen tray. Hold the pen still at the end of a line, circle, rectangle or triangle to snap it clean
  • Select: the Smart Lasso in the pen tray picks ink, photos, stickers and tape to move, resize, rotate, recolour or turn into a flashcard
  • Record: tap the waveform button, then Record. Later, tap any ink to hear what was said at that moment
  • Study: cover answers with Recall Tape, make flashcards, start a Focus Timer, or open Study Helper for a summary and quiz cards (where Apple Intelligence is available)

Frequently Asked Questions

Where are my notebooks saved?

On your device. Turn on iCloud Sync in Settings to keep them in your own iCloud and see them on your iPad and iPhone. If the same notebook changes on two devices at once, both versions are kept.

How do I keep my notes safe?

Notes Pro saves as you write and keeps older versions (More, Version History). Settings, Back Up Now makes a backup file in Files, Notes Pro, Backups; save a copy to iCloud Drive too. Restore from Backup never overwrites a notebook you have.

Search does not find a word I wrote

Handwriting is read on your device a few seconds after you write. Very messy writing may not be recognised. Try another spelling, or search for a nearby word.

How do I draw in 3D?

Open a 3D Space notebook. The 3D Guide shows each move with a ghost finger and Pencil: swipe to spin, pick XY, XZ or YZ, draw, hold still to snap, double tap a line to move the plane there. Example draws a house line by line. You can open the guide again from the menu.

Why don't I see Study Helper?

Study Helper uses Apple Intelligence on your device. It appears on iPhone and iPad models that support Apple Intelligence, with iOS or iPadOS 26 and Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings.

How do I change the language?

Notes Pro follows your device language by default and comes in more than 40 languages. Choose another in Notes Pro, Settings, Language, or in the iOS Settings app, Notes Pro, Language.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Go Pro and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. Notes Pro Yearly can be managed or cancelled in the Settings app, your name, Subscriptions.
